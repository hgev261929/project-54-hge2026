🏠 Home Routine (5kg DBs, 6kg KB)

No lying on back! Use slow tempos (3s down, 1s up).

Day 1 & 4: Lower Body + Abs

  • KB Goblet Squats: 4 x 15-20 reps
  • Bulgarian Split Squats: 3 x 12 reps/leg (Hold 5kg DBs)
  • Single-Leg RDLs: 3 x 12 reps/leg (Hold 6kg KB)
  • KB Sumo Squats: 4 x 15-20 reps
  • Standing Woodchoppers: 3 x 15 reps/side (Hold 5kg DB)
  • Standing Knee-to-Elbow: 3 x 20 reps total

Day 2: Back, Triceps & Core (Underarm Focus)

  • Bent-Over DB Rows: 4 x 15 reps (Squeeze back!)
  • Incline Close-Grip Push-ups: 3 x AMRAP (Hands on chair)
  • Standing Overhead Tricep Ext: 3 x 15 reps (Hold 5kg DB)
  • Standing Tricep Kickbacks: 3 x 15 reps/arm
  • Single-Arm KB Rows: 3 x 15 reps/arm
  • Forearm Plank: 3 x 45-60 seconds

🏋️‍♀️ My Gym Routine

Focus on form and rep ranges. No lying on your back!

🍑 Lower Body (Glute & Thigh Focus)

  • Leg Press: 3 x 10-12 reps 💡 Place feet HIGH and WIDE on platform for glute focus
  • Adductor Machine: 3 x 15 reps 💡 Inner thigh toning
  • Abductor Machine: 3 x 15 reps 💡 Outer thigh & butt shaping
  • Seated Leg Curl: 3 x 12-15 reps 💡 Hamstring focus, squeeze at the top
  • Cable Pull-Throughs: 3 x 12-15 reps 💡 Great glute activation without lying down!

💪 Back & Underarms (Triceps)

  • Lat Pulldowns: 3 x 10-12 reps ⚠️ Drop the weight! Aim for 10-12 reps, not 5. Squeeze lats!
  • Seated Low Row: 3 x 10-12 reps ⚠️ Lower weight, focus on pulling shoulder blades together
  • Tricep Rope Press-down: 3 x 12-15 reps 💡 Keep elbows glued to ribs, pull rope apart at bottom
  • Overhead Tricep Extension: 3 x 12-15 reps 💡 Targets the "underarm" area directly
  • Chest Dips (or Bench Dips): 3 x 8-12 reps 💡 Use assisted machine if needed
