🏋️‍♀️ 3-Day Full Body Gym Routine

Do this exact routine on your 3 Gym days (e.g., Mon, Wed, Fri). It hits every muscle you want to tone!

1. Leg Press

3 sets x 10-12 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
GlutesQuadsHamstrings
💡 Place feet HIGH and WIDE for glute focus. Keep knees at 90° at the bottom.

2. Adductor Machine

3 sets x 15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
Inner ThighsAdductors
💡 Inner thigh toning. Squeeze legs together and hold for 1 second at the peak.

3. Abductor Machine

3 sets x 15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
Outer ThighsGluteus Medius
💡 Outer thigh & butt shaping. Push legs outward against resistance.

4. Seated Leg Curl

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
HamstringsBack of Thighs
💡 Hamstring focus. Curl the pad backward toward your glutes, squeeze at the top.

5. Leg Extension

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
QuadricepsFront of Thighs
Quadriceps focus. Extend legs fully forward, squeeze quads at the top.

6. Tricep Rope Press Down

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
TricepsUnderarms
💡 Keep elbows glued to ribs, pull rope apart at bottom. Targets underarms!

7. Tricep Extension (Overhead)

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
Triceps (Long Head)Underarm Area
💡 Targets the "underarm jiggle" area directly. Keep elbows close to head.

8. Seated Low Row

3 sets x 10-12 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
LatsMiddle BackRear Delts
⚠️ Lower weight, focus on pulling shoulder blades together. Builds back to tighten underarms!

9. Lat Pulldown

3 sets x 10-12 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
Latissimus DorsiWide Back
⚠️ Drop the weight! Aim for 10-12 reps, not 5. Pull bar to upper chest, squeeze lats!

10. Chin Dip (Assisted)

3 sets x 8-12 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
TricepsChestShoulders
💡 Use assisted machine if needed. Lower body by bending elbows, then push back up.
