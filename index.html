🏋️‍♀️ 3-Day Full Body Gym Routine

Do this exact routine on your 3 Gym days (e.g., Mon, Wed, Fri). It hits every muscle you want to tone!

🦵 Lower Body

1. Leg Press Lower Body

3 sets x 10-12 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
GlutesQuadsHamstrings
💡 Place feet HIGH and WIDE for glute focus. Keep knees at 90° at the bottom.

2. Adductor Machine Lower Body

3 sets x 15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
Inner ThighsAdductors
💡 Inner thigh toning. Squeeze legs together and hold for 1 second at the peak.

3. Abductor Machine Lower Body

3 sets x 15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
Outer ThighsGluteus Medius
💡 Outer thigh & butt shaping. Push legs outward against resistance.

4. Seated Leg Curl Lower Body

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
HamstringsBack of Thighs
💡 Hamstring focus. Curl the pad backward toward your glutes, squeeze at the top.

5. Leg Extension Lower Body

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
QuadricepsFront of Thighs
💡 Quadriceps focus. Extend legs fully forward, squeeze quads at the top.

💪 Upper Body

6. Tricep Rope Press Down Upper Body

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
TricepsUnderarms
💡 Keep elbows glued to ribs, pull rope apart at bottom. Targets underarms!

7. Tricep Extension (Overhead) Upper Body

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
Triceps (Long Head)Underarm Area
💡 Targets the "underarm jiggle" area directly. Keep elbows close to head.

8. Seated Low Row Upper Body

3 sets x 10-12 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
LatsMiddle BackRear Delts
⚠️ Lower weight, focus on pulling shoulder blades together. Builds back to tighten underarms!

9. Lat Pulldown Upper Body

3 sets x 10-12 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
Latissimus DorsiWide Back
⚠️ Drop the weight! Aim for 10-12 reps, not 5. Pull bar to upper chest, squeeze lats!

10. Chin Dip (Assisted) Upper Body

3 sets x 8-12 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
TricepsChestShoulders
💡 Use assisted machine if needed. Lower body by bending elbows, then push back up.

🏠 Home Routine (2x 5kg Dumbbells + 6kg Kettlebell)

Same target muscles as your gym day, adapted for home equipment. Do this on gym days if you can't make it to the gym.

🦵 Lower Body

1. Kettlebell Goblet Squat Lower Body

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
GlutesQuadsHamstrings
💡 Hold the 6kg kettlebell at chest height, feet shoulder-width. Sit back and down, drive through heels.

2. Dumbbell Sumo Squat Lower Body

3 sets x 15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
Inner ThighsAdductors
💡 Wide stance, toes turned out, hold one dumbbell with both hands. Replaces the adductor machine.

3. Side-Lying Leg Raises Lower Body

3 sets x 15-20 reps/side
💪 Muscles Toned:
Outer ThighsGluteus Medius
💡 Lie on your side, lift top leg slowly. Add a dumbbell across the thigh once bodyweight feels easy. Replaces the abductor machine.

4. Kettlebell Romanian Deadlift Lower Body

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
HamstringsGlutes
💡 Hold kettlebell with both hands, soft knees, hinge at hips and lower until you feel a hamstring stretch. Replaces seated leg curl.

5. Dumbbell Reverse Lunge Lower Body

3 sets x 10-12 reps/leg
💪 Muscles Toned:
QuadricepsFront of Thighs
💡 One dumbbell in each hand, step back into a lunge. Replaces leg extension for quad focus.

💪 Upper Body

6. Dumbbell Tricep Kickback Upper Body

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
TricepsUnderarms
💡 Hinge forward, elbow pinned to your side, extend the dumbbell straight back. Replaces tricep rope press down.

7. Overhead Dumbbell Tricep Extension Upper Body

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
Triceps (Long Head)Underarm Area
💡 Hold one dumbbell with both hands overhead, lower behind head, keep elbows close to ears. Same underarm target as the gym version.

8. Dumbbell Bent-Over Row Upper Body

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
LatsMiddle BackRear Delts
⚠️ Hinge at hips, flat back, row both dumbbells to your ribs. Replaces seated low row.

9. Kettlebell Pullover Upper Body

3 sets x 12-15 reps
💪 Muscles Toned:
Latissimus DorsiWide Back
💡 Lying on your back (or on a bench/two chairs), lower the kettlebell behind your head with straight-ish arms, pull back over chest. Replaces lat pulldown.

10. Renegade Row Upper Body

3 sets x 8-10 reps/side
💪 Muscles Toned:
TricepsBackShoulders
💡 In a plank with hands on dumbbells, row one at a time while keeping hips square. Replaces chin dip. Drop to knees if needed.
